# Data availability

The accompanying non-identifying source data used to reproduce the main figures, Supplementary Figures and Supplementary Tables are provided in `data/non-confidential/`.

Underlying administrative rooftop-PV installation records, household electricity-flow records, hourly telemetry and household survey records are subject to data-holder permissions and confidentiality restrictions. Access arrangements for these underlying records are described in the manuscript's Data availability statement.

Meteorological observations are obtained from the China Meteorological Data Service Center. Climate projections use NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 products. Additional environmental, socioeconomic and policy sources are described in the manuscript and Supplementary Information and remain subject to their original access and redistribution terms.
