# Data availability

The accompanying non-identifying source data used to reproduce the main figures, Supplementary Figures and Supplementary Tables are provided in `data/non-confidential/`.

Meteorological observations were obtained from the China Meteorological Administration (https://data.cma.cn/), and PM₂.₅ data from NASA (https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/). Solar radiation, land-slope and hourly climate inputs were accessed through Google Earth Engine (https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets). Daily climate projections are available from NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 (https://www.nccs.nasa.gov/data-collections/nex-gddp-cmip6/).

Economic data were obtained from the China County Statistical Yearbook, the China Economic Information Network (https://ceidata.cei.cn/) and CSMAR (https://data.csmar.com/). Search-index data are available from Baidu Index (https://index.baidu.com/). Third-party data remain subject to their original access and redistribution terms.

Rooftop solar installation, electricity consumption and generation data are confidential and cannot be publicly disclosed under nondisclosure agreements.

Household survey microdata are not publicly available to protect respondent privacy and confidentiality. De-identified survey data may be requested from the corresponding author for research purposes, subject to applicable privacy and confidentiality requirements.

The processed county-level daily, monthly and hourly supporting datasets are also distributed as [County_Inputs.zip](https://github.com/ShunlinJIN/Climate-solar-rooftop-adoption/releases/download/data-20261031/County_Inputs.zip). Extract this archive into the repository root to create `data/county_inputs/`.
